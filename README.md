# Harvey AI | Enterprise Solutions & Legal Ops Simulator

> **Interactive Sales Engineering & Value Selling PoC** designed to demonstrate advanced AI legal operations, automated due diligence, document ingestion, and cross-border research for enterprise law firms and corporate legal departments.

## 🚀 Overview
This repository hosts a standalone interactive Proof of Concept (PoC) built to support complex, multi-threaded enterprise sales cycles for **Harvey AI**. It bridges the gap between technical AI architecture (vector embeddings, contextual OCR, zero-data retention security) and C-Suite business outcomes (capacity multiplication, deal velocity, and annual ROI).

## 🛠️ Key Modules & Use Cases

1. **Massive Document Ingestion & Contextual OCR**
   * *Technical:* Simulates secure parsing of 150+ page M&A agreements with AES-256 encryption and zero-data retention policies.
   * *Impact:* Reduces initial review time by **85%**.

2. **AI-Assisted Clause Drafting & Risk Playbooks**
   * *Technical:* Dynamic clause generation aligned with firm-specific risk profiles (liability caps, IP indemnification).
   * *Impact:* Accelerates contract negotiation velocity from 14 days down to 3 days (**-78% time**).

3. **Automated Due Diligence & Risk Matrix**
   * *Technical:* Virtual Data Room (VDR) scanning engine flagging regulatory gaps, change-of-control triggers, and liabilities.
   * *Impact:* Unlocks **€1,536,000+ in annual capacity value** by saving 400+ hours/month per reviewer team.

4. **Cross-Border Legal Research**
   * *Technical:* Semantic vector retrieval mapping EU directives, commercial code, and case precedents.
   * *Impact:* Transforms RFP/client proposal response times from hours to minutes.

## 💡 Technical Stack
* **Frontend:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS (Dark Mode Enterprise Theme), FontAwesome.
* **Architecture:** Responsive Single Page Application (SPA).
* **Hosting:** GitHub Pages.
